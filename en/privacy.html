Privacy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

The app collects no data

Minh Nguyen Studios receives no documents or usage data through Ablagefuchs. There is no account, advertising, telemetry, analytics or cloud sync.

Local storage and text recognition

Document copies and the database are stored locally on your PC under %LOCALAPPDATA%\MinhStudios\Ablagefuchs. Text recognition runs locally with Windows OCR or an installed Tesseract. Protect your Windows account and drive. You delete data, backups and exports yourself on your device.

Exports and other apps

ZIP backups, folder and CSV exports and ICS calendar files contain your data. You decide whom to share them with. Opening documents is subject to the relevant app; purchases are subject to the Microsoft Store.

This website

This local website draft uses no cookies, trackers, external scripts or fonts. For planned hosting through GitHub Pages, the host processes connection data needed to serve the site, such as your IP address. Hosting and privacy information must be checked before publication.

Contact

If you email us, we process your message to answer your enquiry. Please do not send confidential documents or backup files. Controller: Michel Nguyen, Minh Nguyen Studios, Windmühlenstr. 74, 07937 Zeulenroda-Triebes, Germany.
